The Genesis

Veloura was born at the intersection of rigorous clinical dermatology and high-performance textile engineering. We observed a fundamental flaw in the traditional approach to cellulite and skin texture: the industry treated it as a purely aesthetic fat issue, masking it with restrictive compression rather than addressing the physiological root cause.

Through years of research, we isolated the true mechanism—stagnant microcirculation and lymphatic fluid. This realization catalyzed the development of our proprietary Velo-Lymph™ technology, shifting the paradigm from passive shapewear to active, therapeutic apparel.
